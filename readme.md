# Common Assembly Issues on the Formbot Kit

## Fan Wiring on Umbilical PCB

The fan wires on the **umbilical PCB** are **switched from the factory**. When you initially connect everything, you may find that the **hotend fan** or **parts cooling fan won't spin**.

**Solution:** Reverse the fan wire connections to the umbilical PCB.


## Extruder Gear Assembly

The **extruder gear** was assembled **incorrectly by the factory**. The gear teeth must **mesh properly** so that filament can pass through.

**Solution:** Switch/realign the extruder gears so the teeth mesh correctly and allow filament to feed through.



# Quality of Life 

## Umbilical PCB Breakout Board Mounting

Consider buying **2x M3 20mm standoff screws** for mounting the umbilical PCB breakout board to the toolhead. They are way better than the plastic standoffs and wont break.


## BTT Pi WiFi Connectivity

If your BTT Pi can't connect to your WiFi, consider buying a **WiFi antenna with an IPEX connector**. It can be connected on the **bottom of the BTT Pi** for improved signal strength.


## Kirigami Bed Mount

**Remember to print the parts** for the Kirigami bed mount **before assembly**.
